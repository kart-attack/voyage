      How I work

      I start with the brief,
      then go find the people.

      Roughly how every brief moves through the crew.

      1. Read the brief twiceWhat's asked, and what's actually needed.
      2. Meet the peopleUsers, sales, leadership. Everyone holds a clue.
      3. Look wideTrends, competitors, and other worlds entirely.
      4. Find the ideaOne line the whole crew can repeat.
      5. Make, a lotRounds of work, reviewed in the open.
      6. Systemise itTemplates and GenAI pipelines so it scales.
      7. Measure itDid it move the number? Then go again.
